# Relay CRM by ZeroFy ✨

Relay is a sharp, lightweight CRM built for teams who want the signal without the software sprawl.

It brings people, stock, and follow-ups into one clear view, so a small team can run its day from a single desk instead of juggling scattered tabs, notebooks, and spreadsheets.

The goal is simple: give a small team a dependable command center without a steep learning curve or heavy monthly machinery.

## Why Relay exists

Most CRMs are built for large organisations with dedicated administrators, long onboarding cycles, and months of configuration before anyone sees value.

A small team does not need that. It needs the important things in one place, immediately, without a data-entry job disguised as software.

Relay was built to solve that by keeping the workflow small and clear:

- know your people
- see your stock
- move with intent
- run the whole desk from one view

That is the difference between a tool that fits the team and one the team has to fight.

## The operations desk

Relay opens on an operations desk — a single overview of how the business is actually doing.

The desk gives you a live read on the things that matter first:

- **AR** — what is outstanding and what is coming in
- **Open deals** — active opportunities and how they are moving
- **Stock value** — a clear number for what is on the shelf
- **Team activity** — what the team has worked on over the last 12 weeks

One screen, no digging. You can tell whether the day is under control before you reply to a single message.

## Know your people

Every customer and teammate stays close, with context that survives a handoff.

The idea is that every handoff has a home, and every conversation leaves a useful trace. Nothing important lives only in one person’s head, and nothing gets lost between people who were both busy at the wrong moment.

## See your stock

Inventory should never be a surprise.

Relay makes it easy to see what is moving, what is running low, and what needs attention before it becomes a fire.

That means fewer stockouts, fewer rushed emergency orders, and far less time spent reacting instead of planning.

## Move with intent

Loose ends are where small teams quietly lose momentum.

Relay turns them into next steps through a clear queue, so good work does not go quiet. You always know what is waiting, what is due, and what to pick up next.

This is the part that makes Relay a working tool rather than a database you are afraid to open.

## Built for calm, capable teams

The message on the site is intentionally direct: **“Keep every relationship in rhythm.”**

Relay is meant for teams that are competent and busy — people who do not need to be told how to use their tools, they just want tools that stay out of the way.

It is also honest about what a CRM should be. It is not trying to replace your accountant, your warehouse system, or your project manager. It is trying to be the calm centre of the operation, where people, stock, and follow-ups are visible together.

## Free to start

Relay has a **Start free** entry point, so a team can open a desk and get a feel for the workflow before committing to anything.

You can try it with your own data, your own people, and your own follow-ups. If it does not make your day clearer, there is no reason to keep it.

## Why it matters

Most small teams do not fail because they lack data. They fail because the data is scattered and the follow-ups are forgotten.

A good CRM fixes exactly that:

- relationships stop slipping through
- stock problems get seen early
- follow-ups turn into actual next steps
- the team shares one view instead of three

Relay focuses on those things directly, by making the operation visible instead of adding more layers to manage.
