# listing site. 📋

## About Listing Site: A Platform to Connect People & Services. 💻📱️✍️  
I am building a listing platform where people or service provider can list their services, products and other items for sale in the marketplace of your website (like eBay). This is an essential part to make sure that you get more customers by increasing visibility on search engines (SEO) as well.

## How can you list your service?
1. Visit the platform's listing page ([zerofy.me/listings](https://zerofy.me/listings)). 

2. Click "Listing" in the left sidebar or top navigation bar, where they will see all of their listings currently available for sale.

3. Click "Add Listing" button, fill out the form with details about what service or product they offer and provide an image (optional but recommended) of it for better visibility. You can also add any relevant details.

4. Click "List Now" button, and they will receive an email confirmation with instructions for how to promote their listing.

5. **Voila!** It's done. 🎉


## Why should you list on ZeroFy? (SEO) ?
ZeroFY is a platform that helps businesses to reach more customers.
- **Clean**: Clean & Simple. No clutter, no noise - just the essentials for your business'.

- **Detailed Information**: Provide detailed information about what you offer.

- **More Options and Services** : Offer more services or products.

## Pages
- **Home Page** - Showcase ZeroFY listing site.

- **Category Page** - Showcases listings based on categories (e.g., Software, Hardware).

- **listing page** - Shows the details of a specific product or service listed by users (with images and descriptions) in a clear way for buyers to make an informed decision.

- **add new post/service listing** - Allows businesses (and potential customers!) create their own listings on ZeroFY. It also helps service provider list their services/products on ZeroFY platform easily with detailed information about them, so that customers can understand what they are offering.

- **about page** - Provides a brief overview of the company and its mission statement in an easy to read format for potential users or business owners who might not be familiar with.

- **contact page** - Provides a information to contact us (via email, phone number) so that customers can get back into touch if they have any queries.
