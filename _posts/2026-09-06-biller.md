# Biller by ZeroFy ✨

Biller is a simple billing tool designed to help businesses create clean invoices quickly, without unnecessary friction.

The idea is straightforward: make invoicing feel lighter, easier, and more professional for small businesses, freelancers, and service providers who want to spend less time on paperwork and more time on their work.

## Why Biller exists

For many businesses, invoicing is a routine task that often becomes annoying and time-consuming. A lot of tools are either too complicated, too expensive, or too generic to be useful for everyday billing needs.

Biller is a reminder that even simple tools can make a big difference when they are built with clarity, speed, and real user needs in mind.

Biller was built to solve that by keeping the workflow simple:

- create an invoice in minutes
- keep the process lightweight
- preview and print clean PDF-style invoices
- save drafts and track invoice history
- upgrade when you need branding and advanced features

## Make getting paid feel lighter

That is the core message behind Biller: payment collection should not feel heavy or stressful.

Whether you are a freelancer sending one invoice or a small business managing recurring billing work, the goal is to make the experience smooth and professional.

The interface is minimal and focused on the essentials:

- invoice details
- customer information
- line items
- tax and discount fields
- subtotal and total due
- invoice preview

This keeps the process clear and practical, instead of overwhelming the user with too many settings.

## What you can do with Biller

The product supports the basics that matter most when sending invoices:

- add invoice details like name, date, and due date
- add customer information
- list items with quantity, price, and amount
- apply tax or discount if needed
- preview the invoice before sending
- save the invoice draft
- review invoice history

This makes it useful for businesses that want a straightforward billing workflow without the complexity of a full accounting suite.

## Free mode and paid plans

Biller gives users a free starting point with a limited number of invoices before a paid plan is needed.

The homepage highlights:

- no account needed for the first 10 invoices
- free mode is useful for basic billing needs
- paid plans unlock custom invoice branding and PDF printing
- free invoices include a subtle watermark

That gives users room to try the tool before committing to a paid plan, while still encouraging upgrades for more polished business usage.

## Built for thoughtful businesses

The messaging on the site is intentionally simple: “Simple billing for thoughtful businesses.”

This fits well with a product aimed at businesses that care about clarity, professionalism, and efficient operations rather than unnecessary complexity.

The product is not trying to replace full accounting systems. It is trying to be a clean and practical billing tool that helps people send invoices quickly and confidently.

## Why it matters

Billing may seem like a small feature, but it is incredibly important for cash flow and customer trust.

A well-designed invoice helps a business:

- look more professional
- reduce confusion
- make payment collection easier
- improve the customer experience

Biller focuses on exactly that by simplifying the invoice creation process while keeping the output polished and usable.
